Project Overview

This Music as Information project analyzes lyrical language over time to study how themes, emotional tone, and vocabulary evolve across musical eras.

Research Focus

  • Track linguistic and thematic shifts in song lyrics across decades
  • Compare vocabulary choices and recurring concepts over time
  • Interpret results through a cultural and music-history lens

Deliverables

  • Full analytical write-up documenting approach and findings
  • Notebook implementation with preprocessing and analysis code
  • Curated lyric dataset used for text analysis workflows
